# GP-264 — GloPortal data and policy research

**Prepared November 12, 2026 · Parent GP-243 (reusable footer)**

## Executive summary

- **VERIFIED** — GloPortal uses Supabase Auth and PostgreSQL for account and application data. Its homepage sends contact-form names, emails, and messages directly to EmailJS. Those submissions have a separate lifecycle from database records.
- **VERIFIED** — The schema supports customer profiles, vehicles, appointments, service pricing, and shop availability. The admin appointment list reads completed records. The customer dashboard uses sample appointment, vehicle, and activity data. The booking SQL exists, and the public calendar reads availability.
- **PLANNED** — Submitting bookings from the calendar is pending. Its Book Now control shows a coming-soon notice and keeps the selection on the page.
- **NEEDS CONFIRMATION** — Hosted settings, actual email routing, mailbox and export retention, deployment logging, incident ownership, and Garage Glo’s deletion policy require confirmation before publication. The footer currently contains placeholder content.

**Scope.** Findings reflect the local checkout at commit `277f121` on `feature/GP-263-admin-menu-overlay`, checked-in configuration and migrations, historical development validation notes, and current official sources. Hosted dashboards, customer records, and provider accounts were outside the review. **VERIFIED** means supported by source or configuration, with the applicable layer identified. **PROVIDER-DOCUMENTED** describes a published vendor statement, feature, or contract term. **PLANNED** marks functionality explicitly shown as upcoming. **NEEDS CONFIRMATION** marks missing deployment, operational, business, or legal facts.

- **VERIFIED — Browser/API boundary.** The React app creates a Supabase client from a deployment URL and publishable key. Access to protected rows depends on the Auth session and database grants/RLS. The checked-in Data API schema list exposes `public` and `graphql_public`. Project rules prohibit putting a service-role key in frontend code. Privileged dashboard users and server-side credentials require separate access review. Name and phone updates are limited by column grants and owner/admin policies. Vehicles have owner/admin CRUD. Active services and shop availability are public. ### California considerations

- **NEEDS CONFIRMATION — CalOPPA.** California Business and Professions Code §§ 22575–22579 addresses commercial sites that collect personally identifiable information online from California consumers. GloPortal's signup and contact forms make this the first applicability question for the owner. Review conspicuous publication, categories collected and third-party recipients, how consumers can review or request changes if a process exists, material-change notice and effective date. Address Do Not Track and third-party collection over time and across sites as the statute specifies. [California Attorney General guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/cybersecurity/making_your_privacy_practices_public.pdf)
- **NEEDS CONFIRMATION — CCPA as amended by CPRA.** Confirm whether the for-profit operator doing business in California meets the statutory definition, including any applicable entity relationships. Current threshold screening includes prior-year gross revenue over $26,625,000, annually buying, selling or sharing personal information of at least 100,000 consumers or households, or deriving at least half of annual revenue from selling or sharing consumers' personal information. The repository cannot establish any threshold or whether actual disclosures qualify as a sale or sharing under the statute. If covered, review notice at collection, data categories and purposes, retention periods or criteria, applicable rights and request methods, service-provider contracts, and sale/sharing opt-out and Global Privacy Control obligations where applicable. [CPPA statute effective January 2026](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/pdf/ccpa_statute_eff_20260101.pdf) · [Adjusted threshold](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/cpi_adjustment.html) · [CPPA regulations and FAQs](https://cppa.ca.gov/faq)
- **NEEDS CONFIRMATION — California reasonable security and breach response.** Assess Civil Code § 1798.81.5 reasonable-security duties and § 1798.82 breach obligations based on data types and facts. For covered breaches, current law generally requires affected-resident notice within 30 calendar days of discovery or notification, subject to statutory exceptions. If a single incident requires notification to more than 500 California residents, the Attorney General sample notice is due within 15 calendar days of notifying affected consumers. Account credentials, such as an email plus a password that permits account access, are relevant to the statutory analysis. Confirm the applicable triggers and obligations with the business owner and counsel. [California statutory excerpts](https://dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/relaw/excerpts.pdf) · [Chaptered SB 446 summary](https://apcp.assembly.ca.gov/system/files/2025-12/2025-legislative-bill-summary.pdf) · [Attorney General breach reporting](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/databreach/reporting)
- **NEEDS CONFIRMATION — Terms of Service decisions.** Confirm what makes a booking request binding, how staff confirm appointments, cancellation and no-show rules, quote versus final price, responsibility for accurate vehicle information, account eligibility and acceptable use, and how customers contact the business. Align launch terms with the actual booking workflow and any offline shop practices. Have the owner approve the policy and terms before publication.
